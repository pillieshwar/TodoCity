# TodoCity

**Turn any text into a dated task — privately, on your Mac. Then watch your city's lights come on as you get things done.**

Copy a message, an email or a web page and press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> once more. TodoCity writes the to-do, finds the due date and reminds you on time.<br>
A small AI model runs entirely on your own computer, so nothing you capture ever leaves it.

## TodoCity in one minute

1. **Capture.** Copy any text and press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> again — or select it and press your capture shortcut. A card drops down from the menu bar with the task, its date and time already filled in. Press <kbd>Return</kbd>.
2. **Plan.** Your tasks sit in **Today**, **Calendar**, **Anytime** and **Someday**, in projects with sub-tasks and deadlines. Drag a task onto a day or an hour to plan it.
3. **Get reminded.** When a task's time comes, TodoCity tells you — with **Done** and **Snooze** one click away.
4. **Watch your city grow.** Every task is a home in your own 3D city. Finish it, someone moves in, and the lights come on.

Everything runs on your Mac. There's no account, no cloud and no tracking.

## Your city: Lights On

<img src="docs/screenshots/city.png" alt="A city at night in 3D: a building for each project, lit windows for finished tasks, houses at the edge of town" width="860">

> **Every task is a home. Finish it, someone moves in, and the lights come on.**

You're the mayor of a new city. It starts quiet and dark, and your job is to fill it with people — and the only way anyone moves in is when you get something done.

| In your city | In TodoCity | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **An apartment building** | A project | It goes up when you start the project. The older the project, the closer to downtown it stands. |
| **A floor** | A section of a project | You name it: "1st Floor: Travel", "2nd Floor: Program". Tasks on no floor live on the **ground floor**. |
| **A home** | A task | A new task is an empty home with a **dark** window. Finish it and someone moves in: the **lights come on**, and stay on for good. |
| **A faint glow** | Sub-tasks under way | Getting a home ready — paint, keys, boiler — is a checklist. Tick some sub-tasks and the window glows behind the curtains. |
| **Blinking amber** | An overdue task | Someone's waiting at the door. |
| **A house** | A task in no project | Little houses line the edge of town; their porch lights come on when they're done. |
| **"Coming soon" plots** | Someday ideas | Ideas you haven't committed to yet. |
| **"Moving in today" billboard** | Today's timed tasks | On the building's roof, in time order. |
| **A rooftop light** | A finished project | Every home in the building is lived in. |
| **A crane and trucks** | A project just starting | A crane over an empty lot; trucks circle buildings with nobody moved in yet. |
| **Luxury cars** | Your tallest tower | The project you've finished the most of. |
| **Population** | Every task you've finished | Your number as mayor: "Mayor Sam · Lumen Bay · population 198". |

### Milestones: your city grows with you

Your population is every task you've finished. At each milestone the city gains something new — for good. Open your city and click **Milestones** to see the rules, what's built and how far it is to the next one.

| Population | Your city gains |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 🏮 Street lamps on every corner |
| 25 | 🌳 A park at the south end of town |
| 50 | ⛲ A fountain in the park: the town square |
| 100 | 🚆 A train line looping round the city |
| 250 | ⛵ A harbour with a pier, a lighthouse and boats |
| 500 | 🏟️ A stadium with floodlights |
| 1,000 | 🌉 A bridge across the harbour to a second island |

**The rules.** What's built stays built: un-ticking or deleting a task never takes anything away. There are no streaks, points or penalties, and rewards are only for your city — every feature of the app is yours from day one. Each milestone is celebrated once and adds a chapter to the city's **Story**.

**The shape of a building.** Each floor is as wide as its homes need — a floor with 20 homes is wider than one with 4. Floors are stacked with the most homes at the bottom, so every building narrows as it rises; when a floor outgrows the one below, TodoCity restacks them (their names go with them).

**The city is alive.** Cars drive every street with soft headlights, a few trees and bushes line the pavements, trucks come and go around buildings under construction, and luxury cars circle your tallest tower. (Everything stays still if your Mac is set to reduce motion.)

**Explore it.** In full screen your city sits in the bottom-right corner and glows whenever someone moves in. Click it to open the city in 3D:

- **Drag** to turn it to any angle and tilt it, **scroll** to zoom, **double-click** to reset (arrow keys, <kbd>+</kbd> <kbd>−</kbd> and <kbd>0</kbd> work too).
- **Hover** a window to see its task — empty, being readied, overdue, or when someone moved in. Hover a building for how many homes are lived in and still empty.
- **Click a building** and TodoCity glides to it and opens that project; click the houses for tasks in no project, or a plot for Someday.
- **Story** walks you through all of this, page by page.
- See the last **7 days**, **30 days** or **all time**: new residents, population, your tallest tower, finished projects and your busiest moving day.

<img src="docs/screenshots/city-share.png" alt="The share sheet: a picture of the city with its name and this week's count" width="560" align="right">

**Share it.** **Share** makes a picture of your city with its name and, if you like, how many things you did this week. Copy it or save it to Downloads. The picture is made on your Mac and contains no task names, notes or times — and project names stay off unless you turn them on.

Prefer a clean screen? Make the city card small, or hide it in **Settings → Your city**.

## Features

### Capture from anywhere

<img src="docs/screenshots/capture.png" alt="A capture card under the menu-bar icon, with the task, date and time filled in" width="340" align="right">

- **<kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice.** Copy as usual, then press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> again within a moment. Works in every app, no permissions needed.
- **Or a shortcut.** Select text and press <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd> (change it in Settings → Capture & shortcuts) — no copying at all. Or right-click → **Add to TodoCity**.
- **Dates done right.** "by Friday 3pm", "every Monday", "tomorrow evening" become a real day, time and repeat — and "by Friday" becomes a **deadline**. When a phrase is ambiguous, TodoCity asks instead of guessing.
- **Lists become sub-tasks.** Capture a bulleted list and it turns into a checklist.
- **Back to the source.** Each task remembers where it came from — the Slack thread, the email, the web page — and opens it in one click.

<br clear="right">

### Quick add

<img src="docs/screenshots/quick-add.png" alt="Quick add with a task being typed" width="480">

Press <kbd>⇧⌘Space</kbd> anywhere and type `pay rent friday 5pm every month #home`. TodoCity shows how it reads the date as you type. (In the app's own add field, `@Name` also puts the task in a project.)

### Today, with its hours

<img src="docs/screenshots/today-hours.png" alt="Today as a list with a month calendar and the day's hours beside it" width="860">

Today shows what's due and planned for **This evening**, with a **month calendar** and the day's hours beside it: drag a task onto an hour to time it, or onto a date to move it there. A dot under a date means something's planned; a red one means something's late. (In the side panel, press <kbd>V</kbd> to switch Today between a list and hours.)

### Overdue, in one place

Anything whose day, time or deadline has passed gathers in **Overdue** in the sidebar, with a count — instead of a banner on every screen. Move them to today in one click, reschedule by dragging onto the calendar, or tick them off.

### Your week

<img src="docs/screenshots/week.png" alt="Seven days as a calendar with tasks and events, and tasks with no date beside it" width="860">

**Calendar** shows the next seven days, every hour of the day, filling the window — your tasks beside your calendar events, each at its real start and length. Drag tasks between days and times (they snap to 5 minutes), drag a task's bottom edge to change how long it takes, click anywhere in an hour to add one there, and drag tasks with no date onto the week to plan them. Your own meetings move the same way: drag one to a new time or day (or stretch it) and it moves in Calendar too, with Undo. A meeting you organize asks first, since your guests get the new time; invites from other people stay as they are.

**Any time, any length.** Start a task at any minute (9:07) and give it any length: the **Length** chip has quick picks, or type any number of minutes (7, 45, 1h30). Typing "for 30 min" in a new task sets it too.

### Every detail of a task

<img src="docs/screenshots/task-details.png" alt="A task opened in place in the list, with its notes, sub-tasks, date, deadline, project and repeat" width="860">

In full screen, every task shows its **notes** and **sub-tasks** right under it — tick sub-tasks without opening anything. Click a task and it opens **in place**: its title, notes, sub-tasks, **When** (a day, a time, this evening or someday), a separate **deadline**, its **project** and **section**, and **repeat**. Snooze, skip one repeat, pause a series or delete — everything saves as you go, and **Done** closes it.

### Projects and sections

<img src="docs/screenshots/building.png" alt="A project in the sidebar opened to its sections, and one section's tasks in the list" width="860">

- **Start a project** with the **+** next to **Projects** in the sidebar — even before it has any tasks.
- **Open a project** in the sidebar to see its sections, listed like Slack channels ("# General", "# Travel", "# Program"), with how many tasks each has to do. **General** holds the project's tasks with no section. Click a section for its own page: done tasks stay there, crossed out, and **Add Task** puts the new task straight in that section. In the city, each section is a floor of the project's building.
- **Give a project its own color** from its **⋯** menu: its tasks' checkboxes, calendar blocks and dots on the month all take that color.
- **The calendar on the right follows you**: inside a project (or one section), the month and the day's hours show only that project's (or section's) tasks.
- **Rename or delete** a project or a section from its **⋯** menu. Move or delete its tasks first, then delete it.
- **Add Task** at the top right picks the **project**, **section**, **date** and **time** right in the form. The form closes once the task is added.

<img src="docs/screenshots/add-room.png" alt="Adding a task with project, section, date and time pickers" width="860">

Finished tasks go to the **Logbook**. A task added with no date and no project goes straight to **Someday**, where ideas wait until you plan them.

### Modes: Work, Personal, or your own

The sidebar's switch at the top picks a **mode**. In **Work**, Today, Calendar, Anytime, the menu bar and notifications show only your work projects (and tasks with no project); everything else waits, a click away ("4 more today outside Work · Show"). **Personal** starts with every project. Make more in **Settings → Modes**: tick the projects each one shows, which calendars it shows, whether notifications follow it, and which project new tasks go to. Each Mac remembers its own mode — Work on the work laptop, Personal at home — while the tasks stay the same everywhere. Switch with ⌃⌥1, ⌃⌥2…, or ⌃⌥0 for everything.

### Reminders and check-ins

<img src="docs/screenshots/check-in.png" alt="The menu-bar dropdown opened on a task that just came due" width="340" align="right">

- Notifications with **Done**, **Snooze** and **Tomorrow** — or one morning summary instead.
- When a task's time comes, the menu-bar dropdown opens on just that task, everything else blurred. It never takes your keyboard.
- Once a day, it asks you to plan tasks that have no date, one tap each.
- **Repeats your way** — the repeat button sits right after priority: every N days, weeks, months or years, on chosen weekdays, "the 2nd Tuesday", "the last day", or counted from when you finish. End on a date or after a number of times; a deadline always ends the series. Type it too: "every other Tuesday", "every weekday until Dec 18". Skip one, pause or stop any time.

### Connectors

<img src="docs/screenshots/agents.png" alt="Connectors: AI apps that can send tasks to TodoCity" width="340" align="right">

- **Your other Macs** keeps the same tasks on your work and personal Macs — end-to-end encrypted, no iCloud or account needed. See [Sync between your Macs](#sync-between-your-macs).
- **Apple Calendar** shows your meetings beside your tasks (includes Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars added to the Mac), and can **put your tasks in Calendar**: tasks with a day and time become events, both ways. They get calendars of their own, never your default one: one per project, grouped like "TodoCity · Work · Launch" and "TodoCity · Personal · Home", so you tick exactly what to see. New projects get a calendar by themselves. Move or stretch an event and the task follows; delete it and the task goes too.
- **Apple Reminders** syncs both ways — off by default.
- **AI agents** — connect Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Claude Desktop, VS Code, Windsurf or LM Studio in one click in **Settings → Agents**. They can *add* tasks ("add a TodoCity task to send the deck Friday 3pm"), and you approve each one. Turn on **Full access** and they can run your list for you: read, add, change, schedule (to the minute, with lengths), complete and delete tasks, sub-tasks, reminders, projects and sections, through 30 MCP tools, plus ready-made requests (Plan my day, Weekly review, Tidy my Inbox) that show up as slash commands. Everything an agent adds or changes is marked in your lists — pick **Changed by agents** in the **View** button to see it all — and one click undoes it.
- **Agents inside TodoCity** — open the agent panel (the terminal button at the top right of the full window) to run Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI, Gemini CLI or Cursor Agent, or a plain terminal, right beside your list; each one starts with TodoCity's MCP server already connected. **Send to agent** (the agent button on a task's line, or its ⋯ menu) starts an agent on that task in the folder you pick; the task shows how it's going (working, needs you, ready to review), what the agent reports back and any links, and you mark it done after you review. **With agents** in the sidebar lists every task you've handed off, and **What it did** shows the agent's terminal text, kept on your Mac.
- **Watch** (off until you turn it on) suggests tasks from what's in front of you — asks of you and things you promised. Pick who reads new messages in **Settings → Watch**: an agent you've connected (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Gemini or Cursor) or an open model on your Mac. Password managers, Keychain and terminals are never read.

## Moving from Things 3

Settings → **Import Data** brings your whole Things 3 library into TodoCity. It all happens on your Mac: TodoCity reads a copy of Things' database, and Things itself isn't changed, so you can keep using it while you try TodoCity.

1. **Open Things 3 on this Mac** and let it finish syncing. If you only use Things on iPhone or iPad, install Things 3 for Mac and sign in to Things Cloud first.
2. In TodoCity, open **Settings → Import Data** and click **Find my Things library**. If macOS asks whether TodoCity may access data from other apps, click **Allow**.
3. **Check the preview.** Untick any project you'd rather leave behind, and choose whether to bring the Logbook and cancelled to-dos.
4. **Keep in sync** (optional). Leave it on and new to-dos and changes in Things keep flowing into TodoCity on their own. Your edits in TodoCity stay, and nothing is sent back to Things. **Stop syncing** any time, for one project (its ⋯ menu) or all of Things (Settings → Import Data).
5. **Click Import.** TodoCity backs itself up first (Settings → Backups & export → "before import"), so you can undo it.

Running it again later only brings to-dos that are new since last time, even if you've deleted some here.

| Things 3 | TodoCity |
|---|---|
| Project | Project |
| Heading | Section |
| To-do | Task |
| Checklist | Sub-tasks |
| Area | Its projects come as they are; to-dos right in the area become a project named after it |
| Inbox, loose to-dos | Tasks with no project |
| Today, This Evening, Upcoming | The same day (and evening). Today items from earlier days come in for today, not as overdue |
| Anytime / Someday | No day / Someday |
| Deadline, reminder time | Deadline, time of day |
| Repeating to-do | The same repeat when it's daily, weekdays, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or yearly; other patterns come once, with a note |
| Tags | `#tags` on the task |
| Logbook | Done tasks: lit windows in your city |

Trashed to-dos stay behind. **Can't find your library?** Click **Choose the database myself…**: in Finder, Go → Go to Folder… `~/Library/Group Containers/JLMPQHK86H.com.culturedcode.ThingsMac`, open the `ThingsData-…` folder and pick `Things Database.thingsdatabase`. If macOS blocks it, give TodoCity **Full Disk Access** in System Settings → Privacy & Security, or copy that folder to your Desktop and choose the copy. 

## Moving from Todoist

Settings → **Import Data** → **Todoist** brings your Todoist into TodoCity. Todoist isn't changed, so you can keep using it while you try TodoCity.

1. In Todoist (web or app), open **Settings → Integrations → Developer** and click **Copy API token**.
2. In TodoCity, paste it and click **Connect**. TodoCity reads your projects, sections, tasks, sub-tasks, labels, comments and completed tasks straight from Todoist's servers to your Mac. Big accounts take a minute while completed tasks come in, year by year.
3. **Check the preview.** Untick any project you'd rather leave behind, and choose whether to bring completed tasks.
4. **Keep in sync** (optional). Leave it on and Todoist changes keep flowing in every few minutes. The token is then kept in your Keychain; with sync off, it's used once and forgotten. Your edits in TodoCity stay, and nothing is sent back to Todoist.
5. **Click Import.** TodoCity backs itself up first (Settings → Backups & export → "before import"). Running it again later only brings tasks that are new since last time.

**No token?** Export each project with **⋯ → Export as CSV** in Todoist (or, on Pro, download a backup from Settings → Backups and unzip it), then choose **No token? Use CSV files instead** and pick all the .csv files. CSV exports carry your open tasks; connect with a token to bring completed ones too.

## Sync between your Macs

Settings → **Your other Macs** keeps your tasks the same on, say, a work laptop and a personal one — even when the work Mac can't sign in to iCloud.

1. On one Mac, choose **Show a code**. It shows a code like `K7MX-4QRB` for 10 minutes.
2. On the other, choose **I have a code** and type it in.
3. **Both Macs show six digits.** Check they're the same, then confirm on both. That's how you know nothing got between your Macs.

From then on, changes on either Mac show up on the other within about 30 seconds while both are open; a Mac that was asleep or offline catches up within a minute of coming back. Add more Macs the same way.

**How it stays private.** Your Macs share a key that is made on your Mac, handed over only inside the pairing (sealed with a secret the two Macs work out together) and kept in your Keychain. Every change is locked with it (ChaCha20-Poly1305) before it leaves. In between sits a small relay ([`relay/`](relay/)) that holds the sealed boxes until your other Macs pick them up and then deletes them — after 30 days at the latest. It never has the key, so it can't read task titles, notes, projects or Mac names, and there's no account behind it.

- **Keep a project on one Mac.** Under **Keep on this Mac only**, switch on any project — say, a personal one on your work Mac. Its tasks stay where they are and leave your other Macs.
- **Edits on two Macs at once?** Each detail keeps its newest edit: rename a task on one Mac and tick it off on the other, and both changes stick.
- **Away for more than 30 days?** That Mac asks the others for a full copy when it's back.
- **Stop syncing.** **Remove…** another Mac, or **Turn off sync on this Mac**. Tasks stay on every Mac either way. Lost a Mac? Turn sync off on your other Macs and pair them again: that makes a new key the lost Mac doesn't have.
- Notifications, snoozes and Apple Reminders links stay per Mac.

## Install

1. **Download** [`TodoCity-macOS.dmg`](https://github.com/pillieshwar/TodoCity/releases/latest/download/TodoCity-macOS.dmg).
2. **Open it** and drag **TodoCity** into **Applications**.
3. **Launch TodoCity.** It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other Mac app.
4. **Follow the three-step tour.** Pick the on-device model (TodoCity recommends the best one for your Mac and downloads it once), choose how to capture — <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice, or a shortcut (which needs **Accessibility** access, how macOS lets an app read selected text) — and try a capture on a sample message.

There's nothing else to install. TodoCity opens at login and updates itself — look for **Restart to update** when a new version is ready.


**Verify your download (optional).** Every release is built by this repository's public release workflow and carries a signed build-provenance attestation. With the [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com):

```bash
gh attestation verify ~/Downloads/TodoCity-macOS.dmg --repo pillieshwar/TodoCity
```

## Privacy

**Private by default, and yours to open up.** Out of the box, your tasks and everything you capture stay on your Mac. Some features send data off your Mac to a service you choose; each one is off until you turn it on, and says where the data goes.

**Stays on your Mac**

- **Capture.** Text you select is read through macOS Accessibility and handed to the open model running on `127.0.0.1`, with a random port and key for each session.
- **Your tasks** are stored in `~/Library/Application Support/TodoCity/` (**Settings → Backups & export → Show data folder**), with seven days of backups in `backups/`.
- **No accounts, analytics or telemetry.** Notifications are scheduled by your Mac, not a server.

**Leaves your Mac only when you turn it on**

- **Agents** (**Settings → Agents**). Connected agents like Claude Code, Codex and Cursor add tasks for your review, or, with **Full access**, read and change them. They talk to TodoCity over a local connection; what an agent reads, it sends to its AI service. Starting an agent from the agent panel connects it with the access you've chosen, and a task you send to an agent goes to it and on to its AI service.
- **Watch** (**Settings → Watch**). Reads the text of the window in front (never password managers, Keychain, System Settings or terminals, and never what you type) and keeps none of it. New lines that look like to-dos go to the reader you pick: an agent you've connected, which sends them to its AI service, or the open model on your Mac.
- **Sync between your Macs** is end-to-end encrypted. Changes pass through a relay as sealed boxes it can't open; the key exists only on your Macs, in the Keychain. The relay keeps no logs, deletes each box once every Mac has it (30 days at most), and has no accounts. See [Sync between your Macs](#sync-between-your-macs).
- **Apple Reminders and Calendar** sync through iCloud if you pick an iCloud list or account. Pick "On My Mac" to keep them local. "Keep on this Mac only" projects never go to Calendar.
- **Model downloads** you start come from Hugging Face and are checked against their SHA-256. **Update checks** go to GitHub (turn them off in **Settings → General**).

## How it works

```
select text ─▶ read selection ─▶ local model ─▶ resolve the date ─▶ you confirm ─▶ SQLite ─▶ reminder
               Accessibility,     JSON-schema     in code, against     Return to save
               clipboard fallback constrained     today's real date
```

1. **Capture** ([`src-tauri/src/capture/`](src-tauri/src/capture)) reads the selection with macOS Accessibility (`AXSelectedText`). If an app doesn't expose it, TodoCity sends Copy and restores your clipboard afterwards — only if nothing else wrote to it in the meantime, and never when it holds images or files. **Add to TodoCity** in the right-click menu receives the text directly from macOS and doesn't touch the clipboard at all.
2. **Extract** ([`src-tauri/src/engine.rs`](src-tauri/src/engine.rs), [`crates/todocity-core/src/llm.rs`](crates/todocity-core/src/llm.rs)) starts the bundled `llama-server` on demand and asks for exactly three fields — `task`, `date_text` and `date_confidence` — under a JSON Schema at temperature 0. Output is validated in Rust, with one repair retry. The runtime stops after five idle minutes to give the memory back.
3. **Resolve the date** ([`src/lib/dates.ts`](src/lib/dates.ts), [`src/lib/schedule.ts`](src/lib/schedule.ts)): the model only copies the phrase; deterministic code turns it into a day, time and repeat rule. Ambiguous phrases ("next Friday" on a Tuesday, `3/4`) offer both readings.
4. **Store and remind** ([`crates/todocity-core/src/store.rs`](crates/todocity-core/src/store.rs), [`src-tauri/src/notifications/`](src-tauri/src/notifications)) keeps tasks in SQLite and shows native notifications with actions when they're due.

Selected text is treated as untrusted input: it's wrapped and sanitized as data, instructions live only in the system prompt, and the strict schema means the most any text can do is suggest a task, which you review.

## Build from source

**Prerequisites:** Rust (stable), Node 20+, CMake and the [Tauri prerequisites](https://v2.tauri.app/start/prerequisites/) (`xcode-select --install` on macOS).

```bash
git clone https://github.com/pillieshwar/TodoCity.git && cd TodoCity
npm install
scripts/build-llama-server.sh   # build the bundled model runtime once (a few minutes)
npm run tauri dev               # run in development
npm run tauri build             # TodoCity.app and a .dmg in target/release/bundle/
```

During `tauri dev`, macOS grants Accessibility permission to your terminal, not to TodoCity. To use an existing Ollama instead of the bundled runtime, set `PLUCK_OLLAMA_URL=http://127.0.0.1:11434` (and optionally `PLUCK_MODEL`).

**Tests**

```bash
cargo test --workspace   # schema, prompt, retries, downloads, clipboard guard, store, sync, shortcuts
npm test                 # date, time and repeat parsing, quick add, due chips, search and tags
```

### Project layout

```
crates/todocity-core/   Platform-independent Rust: prompt and schema, model clients, verified downloads,
                     clipboard guard, SQLite store, recurrence, Reminders sync logic, Mac-to-Mac sync
src-tauri/           Tauri v2 shell: menu bar, shortcuts, windows, capture, notifications, updates
src/                 React + TypeScript UI: task panel, menu-bar popover, quick add; src/lib holds the logic
relay/               The sync relay: a Cloudflare Worker that stores and forwards sealed boxes
scripts/             llama-server build, icon rendering, UI preview screenshots
```

Built with [Tauri 2](https://tauri.app), Rust, React and TypeScript, [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) and SQLite.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice does nothing | Check that **Settings → Capture & shortcuts → Press ⌘C twice to capture** is on and the TodoCity icon is in the menu bar. Copies made inside TodoCity itself are ignored. |
| <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd> does nothing | Check that the TodoCity icon is in the menu bar and Accessibility is allowed. If another app uses the same shortcut, pick a new one in **Settings → Capture & shortcuts**. |
| "Select some text first" | Select the text again, then press the shortcut. Password fields always stay private. |
| **Add to TodoCity** isn't in the right-click menu | Open TodoCity from **Applications** once, then log out and back in. You can also enable it in **System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Services → Text**. Some apps list it under **Services**. |
| No reminders appear | **Settings → Notifications** shows whether they're allowed and can send a test notification. |
| Accessibility is asked for again after an update | In **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**, remove TodoCity with **–**, then capture again and re-allow it. |
| The menu-bar icon is somewhere you don't want it | Hold **⌘** and drag it. macOS remembers the spot, including across updates. TodoCity starts next to the battery, but never moves the icon again after that. |
| Something else | **Settings → Help → Log file** opens TodoCity's log. It stays on your Mac — attach it to an [issue](https://github.com/pillieshwar/TodoCity/issues). |

## Uninstall

Quit TodoCity from its menu-bar icon, move it from Applications to the Bin and remove it from **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**. To delete your tasks and models too:

```bash
rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/TodoCity ~/Library/Application\ Support/app.pluck.desktop
```

If you used sync, turn it off first (Settings → Your other Macs), or delete the "app.todocity.sync" item in Keychain Access afterwards.
