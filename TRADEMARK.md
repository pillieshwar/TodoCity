# The TodoCity name and icon

TodoCity's code is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You're welcome to use it, change it and share it, including at work and in your own products.

The MIT License covers the code, not the name. **"TodoCity", the TodoCity icon and the app's other logos** are the project author's and aren't licensed under MIT.

- **You can** say your project is "based on TodoCity" or "a fork of TodoCity", and link here.
- **Please give your own build or fork its own name and icon**, so nobody mistakes it for the official app, its updates or its support.
- **Questions?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/pillieshwar/TodoCity/issues).
